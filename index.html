While the meetings are going

A few hours to yourself in a new city, all of it downtown and most of it on foot from the hotel. None of it required.

  • Power & Light District, two blocks north. Bars, patios, KC Live! outdoor stage, and the Toyota group ends up here anyway.
  • Crossroads Arts District, a few blocks south. Galleries, coffee, breweries, and Jack Stack Freight House for the BBQ everyone asks about.
  • Union Station and the WWI Museum, about a mile south. The museum tower has the best view of the city.
  • Casinos: Ameristar and Harrah's are both across the river in North KC, about 15 minutes by car.
  • Hotel pool, 3rd floor, or the room and a nap, if the afternoon calls for nothing at all.

Good to know

  • Dress for the event is business casual, jeans are fine.
